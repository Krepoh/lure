# LURE

Lure is an interactive deep-sea exploration of deceptive design.

Just as an anglerfish uses a glowing lure to attract prey, digital interfaces frequently use confusing or misleading visuals to trick people into making decisions they wouldn't otherwise make.

In LURE, you open a case file at a deep-sea research desk, dive into the dark, and come face to face with a glowing, inviting subscription offer. Fall for it (or don't), then uncover the six tricks it was hiding.

After collecting your data, you're able to use the Redesign Lab to... well, redesign it!

Play
Open [link], or download this repository and open `index.html` in a browser. It's built for desktop.

Features
Case 001: The Anglerfish — hidden costs and misleading subscription offers
The Reveal — the true first-year cost, and six tricks uncovered one by one
The Redesign Lab — six live fixes that transform the deceptive offer into a clear and honest one
Accessibility settings — reduce motion, larger text, reduce visual effects (high contrast), and a clearer font (Atkinson Hyperlegible); full keyboard support and screen reader labels
Cases 002 (Jellyfish: manipulative consent) and 003 (Mimic Octopus: disguised ads) are planned. They would both also use the Redesign Lab.
